Customer pitch

If you were the plumber

“We take care of your Google” — spelled out.

Plain English (what you tell them)

You keep your Google Business Profile, Google Ads, and Local Services Ads accounts — we never own them. You fund ads yourself under a monthly cap you set in writing. We keep your public Google presence accurate, draft review replies in your voice, watch your ad spend against your cap, and send you a written report every week. We do not quote jobs, dispatch trucks, promise lead counts, or touch your money.

$1,800/month · 90-day minimum · one location. Pause or cancel per the agreement. Sensitive replies wait for your OK before anything goes live.

Investor terms (what it really is)

Bounded local-demand-operations retainer: customer-owned GBP/Ads/LSA, customer-funded media under written NTE cap, no performance guarantee, no ad-spend float, no account takeover, no licensed trade work. Fulfillment begins only after six activation facts and cleared payment (`PR2_MERGED_ACTIVATION.md`).

Founder operating manual

For you

Your job is not the desk. Here is what you actually do.

You do (designed)

  • Read the weekly health digest — register, activation states, exceptions, cap alerts, kill-switch status
  • Hold the global kill switch — pauses all fulfillment if something systemic breaks
  • Authorize the big gates: legal name/DBA, insurance quote spend, public site, first outreach, Stripe live
  • Make the first sale (until a sales process exists beyond you)
  • Decide when paper becomes real — nothing below spends or contacts without your yes

You do not (by design)

  • Daily GBP edits, daily review replies, daily ad tweaks
  • Answering the plumber’s customer calls
  • Quoting jobs or dispatching trucks
  • Default exception handler — eight named routes go to operator cells, not you
  • Touching customer ad money or owning their Google login

Named operator cells (roles, not hires yet)

DESK_OPS — outreach records, binary close, weekly compile ACCESS_OPS — Google access, 72h clock, pause on access loss BILLING_OPS — payment facts, refunds, chargebacks MEDIA_OPS — ad cap, media queue, cap alerts INCIDENT_OPS — data incidents EXCEPTION_CELL — only after volume triggers (PR-3)

At launch these can be you + automation + AI agents filling queues — but the rules say they are not “Shawn’s inbox” by default.

Investor lens

For your sister

Why this is not “Shawn becomes an agency.”

The business only works for you as an investor if the operating system is written before revenue, bounded, and falsifiable. That is what the internal desk kit is — activation machine, exception card, dual ledger, weekly health — even though nothing is live yet.

Question she should askAnswer (with evidence on disk)
What exactly do customers get?Five workstreams above with frequencies + sample weekly report — not vague “Google management”
How do you prevent account takeover?Customer-owned accounts frozen in concession ceiling; access loss → pause in 2h; no workaround logins
What if nobody buys?20-shop / 14-day kill test — 0 signed → re-examine, not defend
What if Shawn gets sick?Coverage rules + kill switch; exceptions routed to named cells; weekly digest continues
Where is the money?Dual ledger — demand vs. contribution; $0 actual today; projections labeled paper
What is still missing?Ownership %, your check size, insurance quote, Stripe, site, first signature — all disclosed, not hidden
What she should receive ongoing: dated scorecard, milestones hit vs. ahead, real cash in two ledgers, this playbook updated when scope changes, and the kill-test result when outreach starts.
